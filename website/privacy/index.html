1. Overview

This policy describes how the current Scribe V1 desktop application handles information. Scribe is designed to keep your audio and written content on your device. Scribe does not require a Scribe account or login for normal use.

2. Information Scribe Uses

When you use Scribe, the application may handle:

  • microphone recordings you choose to make;
  • audio files you choose to import;
  • transcripts and word timing data created from that audio;
  • projects and recording organization data;
  • settings and preferences; and
  • Whisper transcription models you choose to download.

These items are stored in Scribe's local application data on your device. Scribe does not send recordings or transcripts to Scribe-operated servers for transcription.

3. Local Audio and Transcription

Recording, imported-audio processing, and transcription are performed locally using the Scribe application, its bundled runtime, and a local Whisper model. Imported audio is copied into Scribe's local recording storage so it can be processed and played back later. Downloaded models remain in Scribe's local application data and are used on your device. Scribe does not upload the resulting audio or transcript for this local processing.

4. Microphone Access

Scribe accesses the microphone only when you choose to start a recording. Your operating system may ask you to grant microphone permission. You can manage that permission in your operating system settings. Scribe does not need microphone access for ordinary browsing of saved recordings or settings.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

Scribe may use network access for limited, user-relevant functions:

  • Model downloads: when requested, Scribe downloads the selected Whisper model file from the configured Hugging Face repository.
  • Application updates: Scribe may check for and download signed application updates from Scribe's GitHub release infrastructure when the updater is enabled for that distribution.

Those services may receive ordinary network information needed to deliver a request, such as an IP address, under their own privacy terms. Scribe does not send your recordings or transcripts to those services as part of model downloads or update checks. Scribe does not claim that the application is entirely offline.

6. Analytics and Tracking

The current Scribe V1 application does not collect Scribe usage analytics or telemetry. It does not use advertising tracking, an analytics installation identifier, or an analytics consent mechanism. No crash-reporting service is included in the current production application.

7. Data Sharing

Scribe does not sell your recordings, transcripts, projects, settings, or downloaded models. Scribe does not share those locally stored contents with Scribe-operated servers for transcription. You remain responsible for any sharing or backup you perform through your operating system or other applications.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored Scribe data remains on your device until you delete it using Scribe or remove the relevant local application data. Scribe provides controls for deleting recordings, projects, and downloaded models. Operating-system uninstall behavior can vary and should not be assumed to remove every item in Scribe's application data. Before deleting or uninstalling, keep any recordings or transcripts you want to retain.

9. Security

Scribe uses local application storage and signed update mechanisms where provided by its distribution. Local storage is not a promise of encryption. Protect access to your device and use your operating system's security controls. No software can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Scribe is a general productivity application and is not directed specifically to children. Because Scribe is local-first and does not operate an account or analytics service, it is not designed to collect children's personal information. If you believe information has been provided to a Scribe-operated service, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when Scribe's product behavior or distribution changes. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Scribe-operated data, contact scribe.teamh@gmail.com.
